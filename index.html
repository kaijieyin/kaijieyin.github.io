About

I am pursuing my Ph.D. degree at the Machine Intelligence Laboratory (MIL), The University of Tokyo, under the supervision of Prof. Tatsuya Harada. Before that, I received my Master's degree from the State Key Laboratory of Internet of Things for Smart City (SKL-IOTSC) at the University of Macau, under the supervision of Prof. Hui Kong.

I am currently a visiting student at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI) from July to October 2026, supervised by Prof. Xingxing Zuo. Previously, from May to October 2025, I was a visiting student at Singapore Management University, supervised by Prof. Zhiyuan Zhang.

Research Interests

My research interests focus on efficient machine learning for computer vision and robotic perception. I aim to develop efficient models that enable hardware systems to perform real-time perception, interaction, and decision-making in real-world physical environments.
